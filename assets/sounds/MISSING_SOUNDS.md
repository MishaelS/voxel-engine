# Sounds still missing

These files are silent placeholders (0.05 s of silence) so the game knows the
names. Replace each with the real sound (any of .ogg / .wav / .mp3, same
name; numbered variants like `fallbig1.ogg`, `fallbig2.ogg` also work) and
delete the placeholder, or it stays in the group and every other play is
silent.

| File | Played when (like Beta 1.7.3) |
|---|---|
| `fire/ignite.wav` | lighting a fire with flint and steel (`fire.ignite`) |
| `damage/fallbig.wav` | landing from a fall that hurts more than 4 (`damage.fallbig`) |
| `damage/fallsmall.wav` | landing from a fall that hurts 4 or less (`damage.fallsmall`) |

Creepers, slimes and squids have no idle sound on purpose: neither has Beta.
